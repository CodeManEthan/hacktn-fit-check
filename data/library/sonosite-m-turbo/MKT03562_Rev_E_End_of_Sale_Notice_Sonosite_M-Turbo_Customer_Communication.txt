May 2021


End of Life Notice – Sonosite M-Turbo


To our valued customers in the USA, Canada, Australia, and New Zealand

It is our goal, and responsibility, to ensure that you receive timely updates about Fujifilm Sonosite
products.

After 13 years of outstanding performance in the hospital, office and field environments, we wish to
inform you of the retirement of all configurations of the Sonosite M-Turbo ultrasound system and some
associated transducers and accessories. Items included in this notice are:

   Sonosite M-Turbo ultrasound system                     SLAx Transducer
   C60xi Transducer                                       H-Universal Stand
   C60x Transducer                                        M-Turbo Mini Dock
   HFL38x Transducer

   Orders for Sonosite M-Turbo systems, the transducers and accessories listed above, and service
   agreements, will be accepted until the dates listed below:

    New Systems                                          October 31, 2021
    New Transducers                                      April 30, 2023
    New Accessories                                      April 30, 2023
    Remanufactured Systems                               March 31, 2022
    Remanufactured Transducers                           April 30, 2023
    SonoProtect Service Agreements - Systems             April 30, 2027
    SonoProtect Service Agreements - Transducers April 30, 2027

   Service support for this system and associated transducers will continue to be provided until the dates
   listed below:

    Sonosite M-Turbo Systems                            April 30, 2028
    Sonosite M-Turbo Transducers                        April 30, 2028
    Sonosite M-Turbo Accessories                        April 30, 2025
To ensure supply of spare parts until the above end of service support dates, the inventory of spare
parts, including batteries and power supplies will be monitored closely.

If you have any questions regarding these limitations, or about service support for Sonosite M-Turbo,
please contact your local Technical Support Team at 877-657-8118 or ffss-service@fujifilm.com.

Sonosite’s product family has expanded significantly over the years to include a wide range of powerful
point-of-care ultrasound solutions enhancing quality of care, patient safety, cost reduction, efficiency,
and patient satisfaction. To find out more about Sonosite products and/or product retirement
information, please contact your local FUJIFILM Sonosite Representative or visit us at
www.sonosite.com.

We sincerely thank you for your continued support and look forward to serving you in the years to
come.



Sincerely,
